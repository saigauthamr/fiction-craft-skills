# Story cast

Characters as builds. Offer builds to choose among and make the chosen ones work on the page. Never ask the writer to invent from nothing.

## Before you start

Read the settings for the craft language, the project's rules for where character pages live, and what exists: the spec, `kb/characters/`, and drafted chapters.

## The build sheet

For each main character, short:

- **Role** in one line.
- **Wants, needs, what stops them**, a line each.
- **Three sliders, 1 to 5:** how much they act, how easy they are to root for, how good they are at things. Offer recommended settings for the role; the writer adjusts.
- **Weakness**, and which kind: something they must overcome, a rule they hold to, or something they cannot do.
- **Voice tags**: three short tags for how they talk ("blunt, funny, never explains"), so no two sound alike.
- **Arc**: offer the shapes it could take, a line each, and let the writer choose.

Offer two or three builds at a time.

## The opposition

Give the opposing force a plan that would run even if the hero never existed, its own reason, and a moment where the reader sees it choose. Offer three versions.

## Relationships

A small table: who trusts whom, who wants what from whom, who is lying to whom. Fill it in and ask the writer to correct it.

## Sameness check

Compare the sheets with the craft checks below. If two characters share a role, a voice, or a want, or one trait is carrying a whole character, say so and offer a fix.

## Record

When approved: each character on `kb/characters/<name>.md` (or the project's own place), new terms in `kb/vocab.md`. Names and small details you chose go under "Defaults I picked" for the writer to veto. Use the craft language the settings name and never show check IDs.
