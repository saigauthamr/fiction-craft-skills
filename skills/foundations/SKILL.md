# Foundations

These skills are about the writer's process: how to plan, how to take feedback, and how to revise. They apply to any story. Load only the child you need.

## Children

**How you work**
- `foundations-chef-not-cook`: learning what each craft tool does so you can diagnose a problem instead of following rules.
- `foundations-gardener-architect`: outlining versus discovery writing, and the risks at each end.
- `foundations-artist-mindset`: keeping story decisions with the writer, and treating early books as practice.

**Feedback and revision**
- `foundations-writing-group-rules`: joining a critique group, receiving criticism, and giving feedback.
- `foundations-revision-guide`: turning feedback or a finished draft into a ranked list of fixes.
- `foundations-alpha-beta-readers`: choosing early readers and using what they tell you.

## Which child for which problem

| The writer says | Load |
| --- | --- |
| "Should I outline or just write?" / "my draft stalls" | `foundations-gardener-architect` |
| "The rule worked for her but not me" / "I can't tell why this scene fails" | `foundations-chef-not-cook` |
| "I keep rewriting for what readers want" / "this draft is ugly" | `foundations-artist-mindset` |
| "I'm joining a critique group" / "the feedback conflicts" | `foundations-writing-group-rules` |
| "I have notes and don't know where to start" | `foundations-revision-guide` |
| "Who should read my draft?" | `foundations-alpha-beta-readers` |
