# Vocabulary

Canonical story terms. For each: the canonical name, a short definition, aliases in use, and where it was decided.

## marker
A salt-iron survey marker. Placing one fixes a channel's route for good. Aliases: none. Decided in the grill.

## channel
The water a ferry travels through. A route is the fixed path of a channel. Decided in the grill.

## the guild
The harbor guild that employs Ines and keeps the maps. Decided in the grill.
