# Story spec: The Salt Cartographer

## Premise
Ines Varga maps the tidal channels of a drowned archipelago for the harbor guild. When the channels start moving on their own, she must chart a route to the one island the guild has hidden from every map, before the guild seals the last safe passage.

## Promises
- **Tone**: wry and tense. Practical people solving dangerous problems with humor.
- **Plot**: a race to reach the hidden island before the passage closes.
- **Character**: Ines wants to be the first person trusted to map the outer reefs. She needs to stop doing only what the guild approves. She cannot have both.
- **Series**: a standalone novel.

## Characters
- **Ines Varga**: cartographer, careful, privately reckless.
- **Tomas Reyes**: ferry pilot and her closest friend, who knows the guild's secrets and is bound by an oath.
- **Guildmaster Oru**: believes the hidden island must stay hidden.

## Rules of the world
- The channels shift with the tide and respond to salt-iron survey markers.
- A marker can only be placed once per channel, and placing it fixes the channel's route.
- Fixed routes cannot be changed later.

## Arcs
- Ines moves from obeying the guild to choosing her own survey.
- Tomas moves from keeping his oath to breaking it.

## Ending
Ines fixes a route that costs her the guild's trust but saves the island's people. The channel map she leaves behind is public.
