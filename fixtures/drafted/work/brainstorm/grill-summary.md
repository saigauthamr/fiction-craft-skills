# Grill summary (confirmed by the writer)

## Premise
Ines Varga maps the shifting channels of a drowned archipelago for the harbor guild. When the channels begin to move on their own, she must find a route to the island the guild hides, before the last safe passage closes.

## Settled decisions
- Tone and form: wry and tense; a standalone novel.
- Character promise: Ines wants the outer reefs, needs to stop obeying the guild, and cannot have both.
- Plot promise: a race to the hidden island before the passage closes.
- Rule: a marker fixes a channel's route permanently, once per channel.
- Soul of the story: Ines choosing her own survey over the guild's approval. The race is the visible goal.
- Ending: she fixes a route that costs her the guild's trust and saves the island's people; the map she leaves is public.

## Open questions
- Why the guild hides the island.
- What Tomas's oath says and who holds him to it.
- How Ines learns the channels are moving on their own.
