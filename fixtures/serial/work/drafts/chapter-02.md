# Chapter 2

The guild office smelled of wet paper and lamp oil. Ines laid the field book open on the long table, and Guildmaster Oru read the page without touching it.

"Redraw it," Oru said.

"It's accurate."

"Then you have measured the wrong day." Oru closed the book, gently, the way one closes a door on a sleeping child. "The north channel does not bend. Draw it as it was."

Ines opened her mouth to argue and found Tomas in the doorway, shaking his head so slightly she nearly missed it. She took the book back.

Outside, the rain had begun. She walked the length of the quay with the book under her coat and did not redraw anything.
