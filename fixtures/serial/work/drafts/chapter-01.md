# Chapter 1

Ines Varga laid the last survey marker into the mud and checked the brass compass against the light. The channel ran north, as it had run every morning for eleven years.

"Same as yesterday," said Tomas, leaning on the ferry rail with a mug of something hot.

"It's never the same." She wiped her hands on her coat. "It only agrees to look the same."

The tide was out, and the channel was a dark seam through the flats. She had walked it so often she could draw it blind. She drew it anyway, in the margin of her field book, and then stopped. The line she had just inked bent west.

She looked up. The water had not moved.

She looked down. The ink had not been wrong.
