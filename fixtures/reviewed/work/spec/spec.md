# Story spec: The Salt Cartographer

## Premise
Ines Varga maps the shifting channels of a drowned archipelago for the harbor guild. When the channels begin to move on their own, she must find a route to the island the guild hides before the last safe passage closes.

## Promises
- **Tone**: wry and tense. Practical people solving dangerous problems with dry humor.
- **Plot**: a race to the hidden island before the passage closes.
- **Character**: Ines wants the outer reefs, needs to stop obeying the guild, and cannot have both.
- **Series**: standalone.

## Characters
- **Ines Varga**: careful in public, privately reckless. Proactive and capable; her relatability depends on small private rule-breaking.
- **Tomas Reyes**: ferry pilot and friend; bound by an oath.

## Rules of the world
- A marker fixes a channel's route permanently, once per channel. Fixed routes cannot be changed by anyone.

## Progress
Soul of the story: Ines choosing her own survey over the guild's approval. Visible progress: the passage narrows.

## Arcs
- Ines moves from obeying the guild to choosing her own survey.
- Tomas moves from keeping his oath to breaking it.

## Ending
She fixes a route that costs her the guild's trust and saves the island's people. The map she leaves is public.

## Open questions
- Why the guild hides the island.
- What Tomas's oath says.
