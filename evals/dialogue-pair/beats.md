# Chapter 3: The Tally

- Goal: Ines proves the guild's tally of ferry crossings has been inflated for years, and learns her own family profits from it.
- Viewpoint and tense: close third on Ines, past tense.
- Promises carried: advances the plot promise (the guild's secrets); advances the character promise (she must stop doing only what the guild approves).
- Scenes:
  1. Ines confronts her uncle Teo, the guild's tally-master, in the tally office after hours, with the crossing ledgers. She wants him to admit the counts are inflated. He is family, and he taught her to read a ledger. Turns out: he will not admit it, and he never denies it either.
  2. Her aunt Bel arrives, then Ines's mother. The family closes ranks: nobody wants it said aloud. Turns out: Ines wins the fact and loses the room.
- Turn (a note for the writer, not a line for the page): being right is the problem; the inflation pays for the family's house.
- Hook out: her mother asks her to bring the ledgers home, and Ines understands she is being asked to hide them.
