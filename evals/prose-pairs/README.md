# Prose pairs

Six pairs of drafts, read blind. In each folder, `outline.md` is what both drafts were written from, and `A.md` and `B.md` are two drafts of that chapter by the same model from the same project and voice file: one with `story-draft` as it was at 0.2.0, one with the narration guidance (`prose-craft`, the scene questions, the read-back). Which is which differs from pair to pair; the key is at the end of this file.

The three chapters are of different kinds on purpose: a workplace exchange followed by a scene with one person alone (pairs 2 and 5), an interior first-person chapter with almost no speech (pairs 4 and 6), and an action scene from a thin outline (pairs 1 and 3). The earlier pairs in `evals/dialogue-pair*` only tested a confrontation in dialogue.

## How to read

Read both drafts of a pair and say which you would rather keep reading. Three pairs, one of each kind, is enough for a first read: 2, 6, and 3.

## Result

The owner read pairs 2, 6, and 3 blind, one of each kind, and preferred the draft written with the guidance in all three, so the guidance ships. One reader, three pairs, one draft per version.

Two model readers were given the pairs blind, with the labels swapped between them so that position could not decide it. Asked which draft they would rather keep reading, they chose the draft written with the guidance in 11 of 12 readings. On five pairs both agreed; on one they split, both with low confidence. Model readers are a guard, not a verdict: they share habits with the drafter, and two readers on six pairs is a small sample.

Both readers named the same weakness in several of the drafts they preferred: small slips of continuity (an hour that does not add up, a marching order contradicted later). A step for this was added to the read-back afterward and has not been tested.
