# Chapter 1: The short count

**Goal:** show Ines doing her job by the book and privately breaking one small rule.
**Viewpoint and tense:** close third on Ines, past tense.
**Promises carried:** character (obedient in public, reckless in private); tone (wry).

## Scenes
1. Warehouse. Ines counts barrels twice. Eleven, not twelve. Tomas says the guild will not pay for a short count. She wants it exact. It ends with the ledger marked as she counted, against Tomas's advice.
2. Quay. Alone, Ines places an unapproved marker in a side channel just to see what it does. It fixes the route. She cannot undo it.

**Turn (a note for the writer, not a line for the page):** the same exactness that makes her the guild's best counter is what makes her break its rule; she cannot leave a thing unmeasured.
**Hook out:** the side channel is now fixed, and nobody knows but her.
**Length:** 1,000 to 1,400 words.

World note: a salt-iron survey marker can be placed once per channel; placing it fixes the channel's route for good.
