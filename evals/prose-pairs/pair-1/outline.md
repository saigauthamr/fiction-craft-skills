# Chapter 4: The second way out

**Goal:** get the crew out of the district and make Ada answerable for one of them.
**Viewpoint and tense:** close third on Ada, past tense.

## Scenes
1. Ada takes the six men through the flooded dip in the Drowned End. Davy Price panics halfway across. She gets them through.
2. At the foot of the old shaft she counts heads. Six, with her. Col is not there.

**Hook out:** someone has to go back through the dip, and it is her crew.
**Length:** 900 to 1,300 words.

Background: Ada Rourke, shift boss, has never lost a man. Davy was in these workings as a boy when they flooded in '09. Col, 16, is her late husband's nephew; she got him the job and told his mother it was the safest district.
